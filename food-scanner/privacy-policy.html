ScanFood AI — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

ScanFood AI helps users scan or search food product barcodes and view nutrition information. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information stored on your device

Food journal entries and dietary preferences that you enter are stored locally on your device using Android app storage. The app does not provide an account system and does not send these entries to the app developer. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data may remove locally stored entries.

Camera

The app requests camera access only to scan product barcodes. Camera access is handled by the barcode-scanning component; ScanFood AI does not intentionally record or upload photos or video.

Product lookup and internet

When you scan a barcode or search for a product, the app sends the barcode or search terms to Open Food Facts over HTTPS to retrieve product information. Open Food Facts is an independent third-party service and its own privacy practices apply to requests it receives. The app does not control that service.

Advertising and analytics

The current app build does not intentionally include advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs.

Children

The app is a general nutrition information utility and is not designed specifically to collect personal information from children.

Security and limitations

Local entries are stored in app-private storage. No internet service can be guaranteed completely secure. Nutrition information supplied by product databases may be missing or inaccurate and is not medical advice. Allergy warnings are informational and must not be relied on to prevent an allergic reaction.

Changes and contact

This policy should be updated if the app's data practices change. For privacy questions, contact the app developer through the contact details provided on the app's Google Play listing.
